AIRIV Indonesia Halal & BPOM Compliance

Indonesia Halal & BPOM Compliance Operations Engine

Product compliance workspace for Odoo 18 with halal status, BPOM registration, ingredient traceability, document readiness, and reviewable compliance workflows.

Odoo 18 CommunityHalalBPOMTraceabilityAIRIV

Core Capabilities

AIRIV Halal & BPOM organizes product, ingredient, supplier, certificate, and expiry context into one operational compliance surface for Indonesian businesses.

Product Compliance

Halal and BPOM status

Track product registration, halal status, certificate numbers, validity, and responsible review owners.

Ingredient Traceability

Source to product

Connect ingredients, suppliers, documents, and product composition for audit preparation.

Readiness Review

Evidence and expiry

Surface missing evidence, upcoming expiry, and compliance actions before release or renewal.

Feature & Workflow Automation

1

Set policy context

Configure compliance categories, responsible users, document types, and review cadence.

2

Register product

Create the product profile and record halal, BPOM, composition, supplier, and validity details.

3

Attach evidence

Add certificates, ingredient evidence, supplier documents, and registration references.

4

Review readiness

Resolve missing documents, expiry alerts, and approval actions before product release or renewal.
